# City Baazar — Rules Document

**Version:** 0.1.0 (draft — built while the owner was away; review the
"Changed from the spec" table first)
**Last updated:** 2026-10-17

City Baazar is YapNPlay's Indian city-trading board game (our own take on
the classic property game; no Monopoly names or art). These rules are what
the `games/citybaazar` engine encodes. Each rule has a short example.

## 1. Players and money

- 2 to 6 players. Quick Play tables have 4 (empty seats filled by the bot).
- Each player starts with **₹25,000** of digital balance. There is no cash.
- Player colours: Red, Green, Yellow, Blue, Purple, Orange (by seat).

**Example:** A 3-player room match deals Red, Green and Yellow ₹25,000 each.

## 3. A turn

1. (Optional) build on complete groups (§7).
2. Roll two dice and move clockwise by the total.
3. Passing or landing on START pays ₹1,500 (once per move).
4. Resolve the tile (§4–§6).
5. Doubles: roll again. A third double in a row goes straight to jail (no move).

20 seconds per decision; when it runs out the turn plays itself (rule 10 above).

**Example:** Red rolls 4+4 from tile 30, passes START (+₹1,500), lands on
tile 2 (Internet) and rolls again.

## 4. Unowned city, transit or utility

Buy it at its price, or decline (no auction; it stays unowned). Buying
needs enough balance.

## 5. Rent

- **City:** land rent; **doubled** while the owner has the whole group and
  no buildings on it; with buildings, the house/hotel rent.
- **Transit:** ₹500 / ₹1,000 / ₹2,000 / ₹4,000 for 1/2/3/4 transit tiles owned.
- **Utility:** dice total × ₹100, or × ₹250 if the owner has both.
- Rent is paid automatically. Owners collect even while in jail.

**Example:** Green owns Cochin and Jaipur (Pink, complete). Blue lands on
bare Jaipur: rent ₹800 (₹400 doubled).

## 6. Other tiles

- **Income Tax** ₹1,000, **Wealth Tax** ₹1,500, **CLUB** ₹500 — to the bank.
- **REST HOUSE** — the player's next turn is skipped.
- **JAIL** — just visiting.
- **Luck / Shagun Chest** — draw a card (§9).

## 7. Building

- Only on cities of a group you own completely.
- Up to 3 houses, then a hotel (a 4th build replacing the houses).
  Each build costs the city's build price.
- Offered when you land on such a city, and any time before you roll
  (Build House/Hotel).

## 8. Jail

- Sent there by a Luck/Chest card or by three doubles in a row. No START money.
- At the start of each turn in jail: pay ₹500 and roll normally, use a
  Jail Pass, or roll for doubles. Doubles free you and you move by that
  roll (no extra roll). After a third failed try you pay ₹500 and move.
- You still collect rent while in jail.

## 9. Cards (each draw is equally likely; cards are not removed)

**Luck:** Lottery +₹2,000 · Traffic fine −₹1,000 · Advance to START ·
Go to JAIL · Jail Pass · Go back 3 tiles · Advance to Mumbai · Advance to
CLUB · Birthday: collect ₹500 from each player · Repairs: ₹400 per house,
₹1,000 per hotel · Crossword prize +₹1,000 · Treat everyone: pay each player ₹250.

**Shagun Chest:** Wedding shagun +₹1,500 · Income tax refund +₹2,000 ·
Medical bill −₹1,000 · Insurance premium −₹1,500 · Diwali bonus +₹1,000 ·
School fees −₹750 · Bank interest +₹500 · Advance to START · Go to JAIL ·
Jail Pass · Festival gifts: collect ₹250 from each player · Property tax:
₹250 per house, ₹750 per hotel.

Moving cards pay START money when they pass START (except Go to JAIL).

## 10. Owing more than your balance

The player must raise the money by selling to the bank at **50%**:
buildings first (half the build price each), then cities/transit/utilities
(half the price; buildings on it must be sold first). When the balance
covers the debt it is paid. If everything sold still isn't enough the
player is **bankrupt**: the creditor gets whatever is left, all their tiles
return to the bank unowned, and they are out.

## 11. End of the match

- The last player not bankrupt wins.
- Otherwise after **30 rounds** (house rule, can be turned off) the match
  ends; players rank by **net worth** = balance + tile prices + build
  prices of their buildings. Bankrupt players rank last, latest first.

## 12. Bot

Every seat can be played by the bot (CPU seats, Autoplay, timeouts):
builds before rolling when it keeps ₹3,000 in reserve (biggest rent jump
first); buys when it keeps ₹2,000 in reserve, or ₹500 when it completes a
group; in jail pays the fine with ₹6,000 or more, else rolls; raises debt
money by selling the cheapest buildings, then tiles outside complete groups.

## House rules (room leader)

| Key | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `set_double_rent` | on | Bare land rent doubles on a complete group |
| `rest_house_skip` | on | REST HOUSE skips the next turn |
| `club_fee` | on | CLUB charges ₹500 |
| `round_limit` | on | Match ends after 30 rounds (off: until one player is left) |
